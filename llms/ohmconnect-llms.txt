# OhmConnect

> OhmConnect is a residential demand-response and virtual power plant company. It notifies households when grid electricity is most expensive or carbon-intensive ("OhmHours") and pays them cash and rewards for reducing or shifting consumption, aggregating those reductions into grid capacity. It serves California utility customers (PG&E, SCE, SDG&E) and operates as a retail electricity provider in Texas via OhmConnect Energy.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the OhmConnect company profile. OhmConnect operates a developer portal, but it is gated behind AWS Cognito authentication and exposes no publicly harvestable OpenAPI, docs, or well-known API discovery surface.
